Why Is My Message Slow?

Imagine this blue dot is your message. It travels from your computer to a website. Move the slider to make the internet path faster or slower.

Meaning of Latency: Latency is the waiting time before your message reaches the website. Small latency means quick response. Big latency means more waiting.

What Is Bandwidth?

Bandwidth is how many messages can move at the same time. Think of it like road width: wide road = more cars can pass.

Meaning of Bandwidth: Bandwidth is the amount of data that can be sent each second. Low bandwidth means fewer messages each second. High bandwidth means more.

Data Sovereignty & Privacy

Data sovereignty means data follows local rules of a country or region. Privacy means personal data is protected and shared only when needed.

Simple idea: Edge mode keeps data close to you (more private). Cloud mode sends data far away (sometimes needed, but less private).

Offline Resilience

Offline resilience means your app can still work when internet is down, then sync data later when internet comes back.

Simple idea: Offline = save data safely on your device. Online again = send saved data to cloud.
